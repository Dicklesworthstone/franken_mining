## 0. Question of record

Which design hypotheses for the assurance chain (Touchstone §20, Bullion §21, DecisionCards §22, Hallmark §24,
agent surfaces §30) survive contact with the real public Barrick workbooks and filings, and with the documented
duties of QPs, CFOs and investor relations through M0–M2? A hypothesis is refuted when it needs data the public
lane lacks with no fallback, when its computation fails on a cell or page, when a binding rule forbids it, or when
a simpler design reaches the same decision on the same evidence.

## 2. The decisive pilot (EVQ-P0-1)

Pane 0 tried to explain, from public pages alone, every per-ounce identity outside its rounding interval: 46
quantities, 75 cells. The operator then re-ran or re-read what the plan adopts.

- **The tables declare their rounding.** 1,207 of 1,212 printed TCC and AISC totals equal the sum of their
  displayed lines (operator census, E815). A total rounded once would foot only about 56% of the time, so the
  tables use the component-sum construction. The five exceptions are three cells: Kibali Q2 2024 (a by-product
  credit printed +1 where −1 foots, in two vintages), Porgera Q2 2024 AISC (two vintages) and North America Q4 2023
  TCC.
- **34 of 46 quantities are explained**: 28 by the issuer's per-ounce rounding note under that construction, 3 by
  Loulo-Gounkoto's public cells (E816), 3 by closing exactly onto their Veladero member (B7 count, not re-run).
- **12 have no public closure** on any construction: Veladero ×6, Hemlo ×2, Latin America & Asia Pacific 2023 ×3,
  and North Mara Q2 2026, which the pilot first counted as explained and its author withdrew in the distillation
  (E819). Each fails a construction-free test (a one-cell cost-of-sales numerator, or a region against its
  members; E817, E819), so state (iv), "undecidable from public data", is necessary.
- **Africa & Middle East Q1 2025 moves from state (iv) to state (ii).** The region's per-ounce rows carry
  Loulo-Gounkoto's sustaining capital (10 $M in Q1) with zero ounces, while its $M rows on the same page exclude
  it. The arithmetic closes; no page states the rule, so the plan marks it *reconstructed*, not *stated* (E816).
- **Side results.** Cost of sales per ounce catches failures that an AISC-only census misses (Kibali Q4 2025: 123
  $M at 1,557 $/oz implies 79.0 koz against 78; E817). Loulo-Gounkoto's loss of control is June 16, 2025 on both
  filed MD&As, which closes W1-DR-03 (E818).

## 3. What the plan adopted

Appendix K.4 of the plan lists the eight rules B7 established; K.3 rows 25–32 list where the text changed. In
short:
1. Bind each value to bytes *and* interpretation (cell or page, header, unit, basis, vintage, raw token).
2. Keep extraction fidelity as its own gate; report identities in five states (consistent; explained, *stated*
   or *reconstructed*; issuer discrepancy with a certificate; undecidable from public data; refused) (§21.6).
3. Declare the rounding construction per table family (component-sum for the AISC tables, E815).
4. Treat the public waterfall as a descriptive bridge, with intervals, signed fallback and the printed-rate gap
   listed (§21.6).
5. Ship public exceptions as evidence-challenge packets, not DecisionCards (§22.1, BD-349).
6. Store public reserves as issuer reserve rows; filed rows win; keep both vintages (§24.3, BD-335).
7. Exact finite VOI where finite states and utilities exist (GB-29 corrected).
8. Agent reads come from the store; an invented id is refused; a draft is not a registration.
