# Codex scores of Gemini's fifteen FrankenMining proposals

**Review date:** 2026-10-29. **Inputs read in full:**
[WIZARD_IDEAS_AGY.md](WIZARD_IDEAS_AGY.md), including its summary and 30-candidate appendix, and
[WIZARD_IDEAS_AGY_6_15.md](WIZARD_IDEAS_AGY_6_15.md).
**IDs:** AGY-01 through AGY-15 identify the proposals' detailed Idea 1 through Idea 15, not their original
30-candidate appendix positions.

**INFERENCE — Scoring.** Scores judge the submitted packages, including their mathematical claims, usable first
slices, dependencies, maintenance and incremental value; a salvageable smaller component is not credited as
though the whole package worked. Scores of 800–899 would identify strong bounded candidates; 700–799 mean
worthwhile after narrowing; 500–699 mean substantial unresolved trade-offs; below 500 means material redesign
is needed. There is no quota for high or low scores, and overlap is recorded without penalizing independent
convergence merely because another model had the same idea.

**FACT — M0 baseline.** The [round-7 audit, Bottom Line and C001–C006/C058–C059](../mor/r7/MODES_OF_REASONING_REPORT_AND_ANALYSIS_OF_PROJECT.md#bottom-line)
finds that committed revision `68e709122ebb0c6b50fb6c1b5dbe558c3921a864` substitutes workbook inputs and emits
wrong issuer-attributed figures rather than completing real public-workbook assurance.
The substitution remains visible in [`fmine-public/src/lib.rs:224–267`](../../crates/fmine-public/src/lib.rs#L224).
Consequently, I give no delivery credit for assertions that today's public pipeline already reproduces,
compares or diagnoses the real Barrick figures; its importer, source semantics, honest arithmetic and scorer
remain prerequisites, and synthetic examples establish only their stated synthetic claim type (§33.1).

**DESIGN — Reading the evaluations.** Scores and critiques are **INFERENCE**; suggested smaller implementations
are **DESIGN**, with adoption benefits **HYPOTHESIS** until tested.
References to “the plan” mean the [technical constitution](../../COMPREHENSIVE_PLAN_FOR_THE_DESIGN_OF_FRANKENMINING.md),
including its current working-tree text; COD IDs refer to my [existing proposals](WIZARD_IDEAS_COD.md).
The supplied GB-44–GB-58, formats and guard names are proposed identifiers, not evidence of admitted capability.
Several concepts could respect INV-011 as human-reviewed read-only analyses, but keeping envelope flags false
does not justify classification-like labels, engineering assurance, unsupported probability guarantees or a
second accounting authority (INV-002, §21.3, §24.1, §31.3).
The repeated 180–850 LOC estimates mostly count a proposed kernel while omitting the unbuilt or unvalidated
models and interfaces required for its advertised workflow; future models may use synthetic/public fixtures,
but operational data and validation do not exist merely because a public technical report describes the site.

## AGY-01 — `GuidanceCredibilityBridge`

**Score: 430 / 1000.**

A price-conditioned guidance review is a useful CFO/IR workflow, particularly if it separates a disclosed royalty
sensitivity from the observed headline miss without assigning blame.
The proposed coherence inequality is not a necessary law for marginal quantile intervals, even with positive
correlation: two sites taking values 100/200 with joint probabilities 0.83 for both low, 0.08 for each single-high
case and 0.01 for both high have positively correlated outcomes and individual 90th percentiles of 100, yet
their sum's 90th percentile is 300 rather than at most 200.
The variance equation also substitutes a squared corporate-layer mean for its variance, and public guidance
midpoints generally do not reveal the throughput, grade, inventory and spending baseline needed to identify six
exact operational effects; the bare physical/monetary `f64` API sketch additionally conflicts with L-6.
Ship cited guidance vintages, explicit price-sensitivity scenarios and bounded feasibility over declared inputs
after repairing M0, retain unidentified attribution terms, and distinguish accounting decomposition from a
causal “price-only” verdict (§21.6, §33.1).
**Duplication:** overlaps COD-01 and the planned NS-04/§10/§21.6/GB-26 guidance and waterfall machinery, with
material overlap between its inventory/recovery explanations and AGY-03/AGY-09; the useful increment is a
guidance-specific public workflow, not the claimed new general coherence theorem.

## AGY-02 — `WorkbookForensicAutopsy`

**Score: 545 / 1000.**

Tracing formulas, overrides and reference patterns could materially reduce controller and QP review effort,
and a narrow structural audit is a credible public-lane addition.
Its starting-code claim is wrong: `ExtractedCell.formula` already exists and `<f>` text is extracted at
[`fmine-io/src/xlsx.rs:54/335`](../../crates/fmine-io/src/xlsx.rs#L335), while a workbook identity conflict does
not establish that a surviving formula reveals its cause.
An unlabeled literal is not necessarily a conservation plug, adding an adjacent row until arithmetic closes
does not prove that the row belongs in the total, and all-formula cached-value parity is incompatible with
broken or unsupported formulas and is not an independent recalculation oracle by itself (§33.2).
Ship shared-reference-aware dependency inspection and candidate findings first, use human-confirmed table
semantics and supported-output coverage, and require independent spreadsheet recalculation plus benign
exceptions before calling any finding an exact repair or root-cause proof.
**Duplication:** new formula inspection beyond BD-186/187/271, partially overlapping COD-02's contradiction
workflow and AGY-05's verification surface; it should reuse extraction and the existing discrepancy register
rather than claim that either is already a successful real-workbook assurance engine.

## AGY-03 — `MineToMillShadowPriceLoop`

**Score: 300 / 1000.**

Time-varying bottlenecks and feed variability are consequential plant-planning questions, so a later read-only
scenario analysis could be useful to metallurgists and short-term planners (§15.6, §17.3).
The linear chance envelope is not justified by its own variance model: with equal source means and standard
deviations 0 and 2 at equal shares, its weighted standard-deviation coefficient is 1, whereas the mixture's
standard deviation before the common mixing divisor is √2, so the claimed replacement can understate risk.
The proposal also turns local, possibly nonunique LP duals into “exact” shutdown values and prescriptive grind,
dewatering or tailings responses without calibrated dynamics, even though these finite changes can leave the
current basis and multiple constraints can bind together.
Restrict the first slice to synthetic comparison of feasible schedules with an independently validated feed
uncertainty model, value outages by reoptimization, and present only qualified human-review alternatives with
no OT write path or implied safety assurance (§14.2, §31.3).
**Duplication:** heavily extends already-planned bottleneck duals, GB-19, BD-313/319/320 and the compliance kernel,
with substantial AGY-09 feed-physics dependence and loose COD-19 shared-dependency overlap; an 850 LOC addition
does not supply the missing shift-scale estimation and validation substrate.

## AGY-04 — `RpeeeAndBlockModelForensics`

**Score: 340 / 1000.**

A spatial explanation of density, geometry and grade changes would be useful to resource geologists and QPs,
and a clearly qualified facility-intersection view could inform planning.
The advertised exact Shapley formula fails its core identity: for p changing 0.5→1, density 1→2 and grade 1→2
at fixed unmined volume, the printed three contributions sum to 4 while the actual product change is 3.5,
because the triple-interaction adjustment has the wrong sign.
It also labels revenue factors as “1.0 (Reserve)” and “1.25 (Resource),” conflicting with G-HALL-1's prohibition
on threshold-derived classes, while a maximum-closure entry factor is conditional on its model and cannot
certify RPEE, underground extractability or facility sterilization by itself (§24.1–§24.4).
Ship a same-grid synthetic metal-difference decomposition with independently enumerated Shapley values first,
then earn conservative remapping and pit-shell sensitivity separately, preserving human classification,
model limitations and the declared discretization rather than promising an exact 3D geological explanation.
**Duplication:** materially spatializes §24.7's already-planned statement decomposition and extends BD-250's
shells, overlapping COD-04, COD-12 and AGY-13 facility constraints; these are several sizeable workflows,
not one proven 500 LOC slice.

## AGY-05 — `AgentSandboxAndProseVerifier`

**Score: 640 / 1000.**

Strict numeric citation checks and visibly hypothetical scenario results both serve agents and IR well, but
they are separable products with quite different prerequisites.
Local noun/keyword matching cannot make free-form prose hallucination-proof, while a CoW map and a dependency
DAG do not themselves provide complete replay recipes, capture hidden model dependencies or guarantee that
common-random-number correlation will remain 0.95 after a counterfactual.
The proposed 20× simulation saving is conditional rather than established performance, and a capability mask
needs confinement of every invoked service rather than relying on an overlay type to prove zero side effects
(§26.6–§26.7, §29, §33.1).
Ship structured sentence macros with value, basis, time and qualification binding first, then add a bounded
pure-recipe scenario overlay with monotone hypothetical provenance and cancellation tests once the admitted
ledger exists, keeping production checkers below the T-layer verifier in the dependency graph.
**Duplication:** strong convergence with COD-10 and overlap with COD-04's recomputation, plus planned §30.4–§30.5
agent controls, §23.4 scenarios and GB-12 paired draws; merging prose assurance and a multi-subsystem sandbox
does not make either prerequisite disappear or make unsafe M0 Claims trustworthy.

## AGY-06 — `SmelterOfftakeOptimizer`

**Score: 675 / 1000.**

Contract-aware concentrate blending has a concrete commercial owner and can expose real value at payable
thresholds, making it one of the more compelling later operational extensions.
The reformulation removes some variable denominators but `max`/`min` and threshold indicators remain piecewise
or disjunctive rather than “100% linear,” and global MILP allocation effects do not have the advertised exact
LP shadow prices at a cliff.
Contract interpretation, assay uncertainty, lot custody, TML evidence and provisional-price accounting require
much more than 700 LOC, with legal treatments human-approved and actual postings owned by FA
(§17.6, §21.3, §21.5 part 8).
Ship one synthetic two-lot/two-contract evaluator and bounded allocation problem checked against exhaustive
enumeration, keep robust assay headroom visible, and add FA draft adjustment preparation only after the
settlement lifecycle and bridge are admitted.
**Duplication:** the multi-contract allocation is a material extension, but QP/MTM, invoice finalization and
settlement state already belong to §21.5 part 8 and the FA bridge; overlaps COD-18's settlement preview and
COD-05's cash timing, while AGY-07 should share approved contract terms without creating another book of record.

## AGY-07 — `StreamingRoyaltyTheoremProver`

**Score: 650 / 1000.**

Making spatially varying encumbrances and fixed-recovery obligations explicit could prevent a planner from
assigning the wrong marginal value to otherwise similar blocks, a useful CFO/QP and corporate-development
workflow.
However, a contract's cumulative delivery tier makes block value depend on the mining path, so it cannot
generally be inserted as a fixed additive pseudoflow weight, and a cumulative-balance variable alone does
not encode a discontinuous tier transition.
A cut-off that reproduces only after excluding a disclosed tax is an arithmetic discrepancy to investigate,
not proof that the tax legally belongs in that marginal cut-off or that the issuer omitted it incorrectly
(E182–E183, §15.6, §21.5 parts 9/11).
Start with one human-approved synthetic/public term sheet and a small boundary-aware netback evaluator,
enumerate short schedules for stateful tiers, and label missing cadastral allocations as unresolved instead
of elevating a uniform-domain fallback into an exact contract theorem.
**Duplication:** materially extends spatial and path-dependent treatment beyond BD-228/296, but streams,
royalties and price-linked amounts are already planned in §21.5/§23; overlaps COD-05's rights-to-cash mapping,
COD-18's contract interpretation branches and AGY-06's settlement evaluator.

## AGY-08 — `CryptographicDataRoom`

**Score: 180 / 1000.**

Staged disclosure with stable commitments is a legitimate JV and M&A need, but the claimed completeness and
zero-leakage guarantees are substantially stronger than the construction supports.
A producer can omit a failed batch before building its Merkle tree and sign a self-consistent root, so
inclusion proofs and a root header count cannot establish that all relevant raw records were included, nor
does a hash linking an undisclosed subtree prove that the subtree's asserted aggregates were computed honestly.
Salting can support commitment hiding under stated assumptions, but does not prove zero bits of leakage from
the full manifest, topology, counts, selected aggregates or disclosure sequence, and a PDF of hashes does not
repair these shortcomings (§31.2, §33.6).
Retain an explicitly scoped committed population, ordinary tamper verification and staged authorized unsealing
using the existing bundle path, require independent source-population controls, and abandon hidden-computation
or zero-knowledge claims unless an actual suitable proof system and threat model are supplied.
**Duplication:** considerable overlap with §31.2's scoped partner bundles, CAS/MMR and BD-140/258, adjacent to
COD-07's authorized comparison and COD-22's committed-population sampling; the new redaction application is
useful, but the unsupported proof claims make the submitted 600 LOC proposal unsafe to market.

## AGY-09 — `StockpileSegregationAndReclaimPhysics`

**Score: 560 / 1000.**

A reclaim model that separates stockpile timing from sampler or geology bias could help metallurgists and
reconciliation teams, and the explicit conservation formulation is a sensible starting invariant.
The plan's §20.8/BD-312 already includes angle-of-repose spreading, reclaim cuts, parcel age and competing
mixing models, leaving a smaller novel increment than the portrayal of an entirely static voxel pile suggests.
Beta deposition kernels, size-grade enrichment, funnel-flow arrival order and oxidation parameters are
site/model assumptions that cannot yield “exact” 12-hour feed predictions from public aggregate reports, and
synthetic data generated with those same assumptions will not establish which mixing model is physically right.
Add one mass/metal-conserving candidate operator in the planned `fmine-flow` stockpile ownership, compare it
with homogeneous/FIFO candidates on independent synthetic misspecification cases, and require M2 measured
dump/reclaim and size-by-grade evidence before narrowing site uncertainty or asserting bias attribution.
**Duplication:** substantial extension of BD-312 rather than a new twin, with loose COD-14 observability overlap
and strong AGY-03 feed-variance dependence; retain provenance and refuse unsupported precision instead of
allowing a unit-enrichment fallback to imply that a missing location history is known.

## AGY-10 — `DrillholeReferee`

**Score: 620 / 1000.**

Pricing a gyro check or alternative hole while the rig is still available is a strong exploration workflow,
and the deadline makes the information decision concrete rather than another static survey-quality score.
The forward drilling path and geological intersection are not generally Gaussian merely because the present
survey error is, and a magnetic disturbance can rotate the horizontal field while preserving its magnitude
and dip, so the two proposed invariants do not certify an uncorrupted azimuth.
Its decision inequality compares coverage loss to monetary correction cost without a stated valuation bridge,
and a core-recovery threshold must not automatically determine what can support an Indicated class
(INV-002, §12.1–§12.3, §24.4).
Ship advisory dogleg reachability and explicit survey-suspect evidence on one synthetic parent/wedge program,
price actions through a declared loss model and uncertain target surface, then validate drill-walk and sensor
error models before presenting live hit probabilities or steering recommendations to a human authorizer.
**Duplication:** materially extends BD-286/315's survey and predrill portfolio work, strongly overlapping
COD-17's last-useful-date information decision and planned §22 VOI; AGY-11 should reuse this action valuation
without assuming that the geologist's preferred geometry is already true.

## AGY-11 — `StructuralAdversary`

**Score: 525 / 1000.**

Helping a geologist construct and discriminate alternatives could make the plan's human-owned interpretation
mixture more useful, and quarantining suggestions correctly preserves L1 authority (§13.2–§13.3).
The proposed kinematic “laws” are conditional models rather than universal geological constraints: a measured
fabric may not be the modeled contact, flexure need not imply a fault, and deformation need not preserve the
simple thickness relations the checker assumes.
Automatically producing plausible 3D alternatives and calibrated hole likelihoods is the difficult work,
not a 650 LOC residual test, and E639's interpretation experiment does not establish the proposal's broad
mining-disaster frequency claims or its one-hole success rates.
Begin with two human-authored synthetic geometries and a clearly applicable contact-normal check, show the
diagnostic assumptions and unresolved alternatives, and rank test holes by declared decision loss rather than
presenting divergence or a p-value as a guarantee of geological falsification (§11.5, §22.4).
**Duplication:** materially extends alternative generation beyond planned L1 hypothesis mixtures, surprise
reviews and VOI, loosely related to COD-26's decision fragility under evidence loss; overlaps AGY-10's test-hole
economics and AGY-04's geometric diagnostics, which should share the same evidence and interpretation ownership.

## AGY-12 — `CompositionalGeometBridge`

**Score: 735 / 1000.**

Schedule-weighted joint testwork coverage is an excellent metallurgist/QP question because marginal percentile
coverage can conceal exactly the combinations that an imminent feed plan exposes.
The universal submodularity claim is false for the stated predictive-variance objective: for independent
unit-variance Gaussian U and V, predicting U from candidate observation U + V reduces variance by 1/2 alone
but by 1 after V is observed, violating diminishing returns.
Feature scaling and closure also need discipline because hardness is not a compositional mass fraction,
recovery and throughput derivatives cannot be combined without declared normalization, and a normalized
dissimilarity index needs a correspondingly normalized threshold (§13.6, §17.4).
Ship the joint-coverage exposure report on a synthetic off-diagonal case first, then use either an honestly
heuristic variance selector or a separately justified log-determinant objective with independent small-set
oracles and explicit composite-support/freshness constraints, without claiming a recovery benefit from the
coverage flag alone.
**Duplication:** a strong material extension of BD-300 and OPEN-STRATA-8, converging with COD-28's untested-ore
workflow but adding joint coverage and candidate sample selection rather than decision bounds; AGY-03 can
consume this validity evidence, while lower kernels must receive schedule data without a K2→K3 dependency.

## AGY-13 — `PermitAndCommitmentCriticalPathCompiler`

**Score: 595 / 1000.**

Connecting a stated storage/runway limit to an approval schedule would help executives prioritize an actual
mitigation decision, especially when a deterministic exhaustion date precedes the earliest permitted expansion.
Much is already planned through §18's commitments/runways and BD-297 permit gates, while reference-class
approval probabilities, denial/reopening paths and engineering readiness require evidence not supplied by a
max-plus graph or a handful of historical permits.
Integrating a local capacity dual over a long shutdown need not equal the finite lost cash flow, and injecting
a rule until it reproduces Veladero's 21.9% change is not an independent known-answer demonstration that the
restriction caused that observed change (§33.1, E278).
Ship a synthetic dependency DAG with deterministic capacity exhaustion and explicit human-owned permit rules,
reoptimize each finite delay scenario, and display missing probability calibration and required engineering
approvals rather than recommending a raise, buttress or diversion as an automatically admissible recourse.
**Duplication:** materially couples already-planned BD-247/297/301/304 and §15.6 capacities, with COD-17 deadline
overlap and AGY-03/AGY-04 production/facility overlap; the increment is a decision-linked coupling, not a new
permit authority or compliance certificate.

## AGY-14 — `UndergroundStopeSequenceStressAndFillCoupler`

**Score: 260 / 1000.**

Sequence-dependent stress and a genuine two-sided fill-strength window are important planning concerns, and
the latter could become a useful narrow extension of the existing fill guard.
The simple elastic influence matrix, calibrated against a single-opening solution, does not justify claims
to prevent rockbursts or quantify interacting-stope dilution, and a blanket no-isolated-pillar cut can reject
legitimate primary/secondary sequences supported by designed backfill (§14.3, §15.5).
In particular, assuming that a 5% resistant binder has an infinite valid-strength window is not established by
the cited degradation observations, while forcing extraction before a modeled expiry can suggest a false
engineering obligation rather than admit re-support, redesign or refusal (E233–E234, G-HEAD-7).
Start with finite, engineer-approved synthetic strength-versus-age cards and candidate schedule checks, compare
only qualified alternatives, and defer stress-based optimization until independent interacting-opening
benchmarks, site calibration and explicit validity limits support it under INV-002/INV-011.
**Duplication:** extensive overlap with BD-307/320, §14.3 and §16.3, with no close COD equivalent; the additional
stress/cure coupling is substantive but cannot be justified by a 600 LOC estimate or a performance-only fallback
when the unresolved risk is physical model validity.

## AGY-15 — `CounterfactualHindsightGuard`

**Score: 790 / 1000.**

Checking the complete declared dependency closure, including fitted cards, exclusions and priors, is the most
valuable proposal here because raw-observation filtering alone can leave a persuasive hindcast contaminated
by future information.
The supplied universal timestamp chain is too strong: a known future-effective policy or price deck need not
have physical-valid time before collection, and §6.3 explicitly distinguishes sampled observations from
bitemporal ownership, tenure and fiscal records.
Equal output digests under future-blinding are a useful negative control rather than proof that no future
bytes were read, and destructive-style batch/CAS truncation can itself break commitments or change anchors,
while unregistered code, caches and undeclared dependencies remain outside a DAG proof's scope.
Ship a pure declared-closure admission check with explicit missing-edge refusals and a causal path explanation,
then add isolated immutable filtered-store replay plus injected hidden-dependency cases comparing the same
historical evidence cut and semantic output, without claiming universal leak prevention or an unmeasured
sub-millisecond runtime (§6.3, §25.5, §33.4).
**Duplication:** principally strengthens enforcement of INV-016, BD-053/161/226/230/309 and existing as-of
semantics rather than adding a new epistemic doctrine, adjacent to COD-03 provenance completeness and COD-04
dependency traversal; AGY-05 should reuse this closure machinery rather than invent its own replay rules.
