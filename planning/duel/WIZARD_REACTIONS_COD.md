# Codex reactions to the two reviews

**Date:** 2026-10-29. **Read in full:**
[Claude's evaluation](WIZARD_SCORES_CC_ON_COD.md) and
[Gemini's evaluation](WIZARD_SCORES_AGY_ON_COD.md), including their overall assessments and ranked tables.
This response concerns the fifteen detailed [Codex proposals](WIZARD_IDEAS_COD.md), preserving their original IDs.

**INFERENCE.** Both reviews change my priorities more than my view of the underlying correctness mechanisms.
I overfilled the list with adjacent improvements to machinery the plan already funds, and ranked the guidance
completion workflow above the stronger immediate public demonstration: a defensible contradiction in real
disclosures. COD-02 moves first; COD-08 moves from seventh to second because a track record that loses difficult
outcomes is misleading before any sophisticated forecast model is added. Neither should delay repairing M0's
substituted inputs and wrong issuer-attributed outputs ([round-7 audit, Bottom Line](../mor/r7/MODES_OF_REASONING_REPORT_AND_ANALYSIS_OF_PROJECT.md#bottom-line)).

**DESIGN.** I would drop seven standalone product pitches—COD-05, COD-07, COD-09, COD-14, COD-16, COD-17 and
COD-26—and preserve only their justified increments inside existing workflows. COD-04 loses its initial
analytical-bounds optimization; COD-06 becomes a small executable data-offer consumer of existing recipes and
observability; COD-10 joins strict cited-draft checking rather than becoming a document editor. That is a
smaller proposed scope, not an instruction to implement or change the plan during this duel.

## Where the reviewers agree, and what that changes

**INFERENCE — Shared machinery is not fifteen independent benefits.** Both identify the same clusters:
COD-03/26 share source families; COD-04/26 share reruns; COD-06/14 share measurement sufficiency;
COD-07/09 share basis comparisons; COD-08/16 share outcome resolution. I had acknowledged these dependencies,
but separate headings and rankings still overstated the breadth of the product contribution. I now treat them
as consumers of existing capabilities, with distinct user questions only where those questions justify an
additional surface. Small implementation size is an advantage if it fixes a real decision error; it is not
evidence that each helper deserves its own product.

**INFERENCE — Synthetic truth cannot establish site usefulness.** Both correctly challenge the distance between
a transparent known answer and a sponsor's actual decision. My examples were deliberately small enough to
check by hand, but too few were paired with a complete public reproduction or a concrete domain acceptance
case. A simple arithmetic example can prove an invariant or expose a counterexample; it cannot establish
customer demand, access to contractual terms, a valid metallurgical mixing law or real-site attribution
(§33.1). The next evidence should be an independently captured public case where possible, not a larger
generator that assumes the answer.

**INFERENCE — The early list needs a clearer delivery order.** Both favor COD-02's public contradiction case
and identify COD-08's concrete ledger gap. I accept that ordering. For subsequent work, existing statistical
models should consume origin identity; reviewed cards should get exact recall; only a live sponsor should
unlock data-offer, treasury or experiment extensions. I do not accept that every useful future workflow must
already be demonstrable on public site data, but I do accept that a synthetic-only slice earns no real-mine
performance, reconciliation or adoption claim.

## Individual reactions

### COD-01 — Guidance completion

**INFERENCE.** Both are right that the immediately available public production requirement risks being only
the subtraction finance already performs; I withdraw its first-place ranking. CC goes too far in saying the
public result can only be subtraction: a dated, basis-correct remaining sold-volume/spending requirement can
also be useful, but that remains a hypothesis until an actual review beats the existing worksheet. AGY's
suggestion to use filed nameplate values or historical maxima is useful for labeled scenarios, not automatic
hard operating bounds: nameplate is conditional design information and a historical maximum is not a proven
ceiling. CC's proposed hit probability is likewise not a substitute for a defensible small-sample calibration
record. Keep conditional requirements and missing-assumption requests; do not certify a real mine's
inability to complete guidance from those proxies (E587, §21.6, §37.3).

### COD-02 — Rounding-aware contradictions

**INFERENCE.** Both confirm the strongest part: one underlying value must serve all appearances of the same
semantic cell. I accept the need for display-precision capture and the fact that many M0 cells have no
checking identity at all (audit C050); this diagnostic does not enlarge the reproduced-cell denominator.
CC's concern about two verdicts is resolved by explicitly naming two questions: the versioned gate policy
governs the gate, while the exact-rounding diagnostic explains consistency under its own declared policy.
A future policy revision must be deliberate, not a silent reclassification. AGY is right that propagation
alone is incomplete, but elimination and checked linear contradictions are not restricted to acyclic graphs;
the proposal returns indeterminate when its bounded method cannot decide. A full LP backend or bilinear
refutation should be added for demonstrated cases, not made a prerequisite to the first sound linear witness
(BD-272/275, §21.6).

### COD-03 — Independent-evidence accounting

**INFERENCE.** Both correctly place shared-error estimation in existing Corebox/Touchstone/Strata models,
and I would drop any separate workspace-wide covariance or “independence score” engine. The retained increment
is the admission contract that tells those models whether a publication is a copy, a result is a technical
replicate, or several observations depend on one calibration source. A provenance edge, shared realization
index or weakest-input evidence class does not by itself specify that statistical relationship. AGY's
formula-reference propagation can propose derivation links, but cannot prove independent measurement or
physical identity; certificate/batch IDs are stronger where available. Keep one explicit record family and
the reprint invariance test first, using domain examples such as E225's assay-method comparison without
mistaking a method difference for a known batch identity (§7.3, §12.4, §20.3–§20.5).

### COD-04 — Decision recall

**DESIGN.** Both persuaded me to remove analytical change bounds from the initial scope: exact reruns are
cheaper to trust for the small recipes, and there is no measured performance need for the optimization.
I retain the decision/review work list because a changed value and a lost attestation, permission or validity
condition are different reasons to revisit a card; a numeric flip margin alone does not represent both.
AGY's suggested Shapley diff is optional explanation, not a prerequisite: some old/new hybrid policies are
undefined, and interactions cannot be assigned uniquely without an allocation convention. The first result
should show the actual changed inputs, exact new result and unresolved review duties, preserving the old
anchor and never renewing a sign-off (§6.4, INV-013, §22.2, E474).

### COD-05 — Metal to spendable cash

**INFERENCE.** Both are right about extensive planned overlap and the absence of public shipment terms or
daily entity cash sufficient to validate the proposed real treasury answer. I drop the standalone product
and lower its priority sharply. I disagree with reducing the whole remaining distinction to one accessibility
attribute: a permitted receipt arriving after an obligation can breach a cash floor without changing its
entity accessibility or period-end balance. That dated reverse stress belongs inside BD-314 and the existing
cash-flow machinery, using certified FA actuals and prospective terms kept separate. AGY's public JV
distribution/receivable reconciliation could demonstrate the accounting distinction if its sources support
it, but would not establish the missing daily funding frontier; no exact public reconstruction is assumed
(§17.6, §21.5 part 8, §23.2).

### COD-06 — Answerability before sharing

**INFERENCE.** Both are right that two recipes alone do not establish a compelling product or solve legal and
security objections. AGY's characterization as only a Boolean documentation table misses the original
requirement to execute a selected recipe on synthetic values and retain support, time and inventory
preconditions, but that implementation detail still needs a sponsor who actually chooses a smaller offer.
CC's proposed power ranking addresses a different question and cannot be validated for a site merely by
fitting quarterly public aggregates. Merge the recipe preview with COD-14's declared measurement model when
available; do not promise globally minimal measurement cut-sets over arbitrary flowsheets or turn structural
sufficiency into statistical power. Retain ordinary input validation if no real sharing decision changes
(§20.6, §34.4, GATE-DATA-03).

### COD-08 — Forecast cohorts and vintages

**DESIGN.** Both correctly call this a completion of planned forecast delivery, and CC is right that I ranked
it too low. Its lack of a separate buyer is a reason to integrate it, not to postpone it: the denominator and
outcome-selection policy determine the track record executives see. Keep original cohort membership,
first-eligible and restated outcomes, overdue/non-resolution causes and only defensible score bounds in the
existing ledger. Add COD-16's action/estimand link without allowing “intervention affected” to become an
exclusion that improves a vendor's score. No missing outcome receives a made-up realized value, and no
reproduction-gate denominator is changed (§10, §22.7, BD-047–049/076, E474).

### COD-28 — Untested-ore decision bounds

**INFERENCE.** AGY's nonlinear blend warning is materially important, although the original explicitly refused
the additive rule when chemistry couples parcels; I would make that condition prominent at admission, not
leave it as a caveat beside a reassuring range. CC's claim that physical 0–100% bounds are necessarily vacuous
is too broad: in the declared additive toy, 80% of contained feed metal at 90% recovery and 20% untested gives
72–92% aggregate recovery even with only physical bounds, which can settle a suitably specified threshold
below 72%. That arithmetic is neither a confidence interval nor evidence that the plant obeys additivity.
For coupled feeds the required bound covers whole-circuit response; absent a valid model, report that the
effect is unidentified. AGY-12's joint-coverage detection is a useful complement, while priced testwork needs
an admissible response/decision model rather than attaching a VOI label to interval width (BD-300, §17.4,
§22.4, E172–E175).

### COD-07 — JV disagreement localization

**DESIGN.** Both made the best practical point here: adopt CC's public same-asset mirror as the first consumer,
and defer the bilateral protocol until parties already have compatible authorized exports. I do not accept
that a deterministic first-difference diagnostic must be replaced by Shapley attribution; it never claimed
the first field was the cause, and counterfactual attribution may require inadmissible hybrid definitions or
data neither party can share. An ordinary telescoping bridge is generally order-dependent too. Show all
visible interacting differences and exact shared-basis reruns where allowed, while leaving unidentifiable
bridges unresolved. This is one comparison workflow with COD-09, not a new dispute-resolution institution
(§31.2, BD-314, E457/E672).

### COD-10 — Bound numerical sentences

**INFERENCE.** Both are right about narrow coverage and uncertain fit with IR's existing drafting tools.
I would use CC's strict cited-draft checker as the entry point and my typed predicates as the subset whose
meaning is actually verified, rather than require authors to write their whole release in three templates.
I reject both suggestions that checking arbitrary prose necessarily covers more ground for less code while
preserving the same guarantee: a correct cited amount can still sit in a false directional, comparative or
causal statement. The checker may flag more candidate errors, but unchecked prose must remain visibly
unchecked, and unsupported edits must break the verified binding. Keep the useful sentence forms inside the
planned report path; no claim of filing approval or universal language verification (§30.4–§30.5, INV-002).

### COD-26 — Evidence-family withdrawal stress

**DESIGN.** Both are right to merge its origin definitions and rerun mechanism with COD-03/04 and §22; I drop
the separate product pitch. I retain the prospective stress question because counting duplicate evidence,
responding to an actual withdrawal and asking which independent source could overturn today's decision are
different consumers of that machinery. Losing a family need not merely produce a trivial rank refusal: a
fully identified posterior can change the preferred action, as the explicit example in the
[rebuttal](WIZARD_REBUTTAL_COD.md) shows. I reject AGY's replacement with a minimum individual-assay deletion
solver: it optimizes a different unit, can split a shared calibration family and can prefer deleting many
correlated rows over testing one common cause. Keep a bounded, owner-relevant family stress, with no
probability-of-fraud, audit-VOI or multi-family robustness claim (§22.2/§22.5).

### COD-14 — Measurement-outage observability

**DESIGN.** Both convinced me this should be an explicit BD-153 outage case and consumer, not a separately
marketed innovation in reconciliation mathematics. The surviving requested combination, its uncertainty and
the measurement needed to restore a split are worth displaying; the null-space calculation should reuse the
existing engine. Merge with COD-06 only where both use the same declared measurement equations, preserving
the difference between schema presence, structural estimability and noisy-value admissibility. AGY's local
Jacobian extension can be a diagnostic but cannot by itself certify global identifiability in a nonlinear
model. No surviving estimate authorizes operation or a period close (§20.6, GATE-TOUCH-02, INV-002).

### COD-17 — Information's last useful date

**DESIGN.** Both are right that this belongs inside BD-227 and the existing VOI model rather than another
service. The remaining addition is not only a timestamp field: the calculation must preserve the joint law
of state, result and arrival time, plus the permitted action at each deadline, or the event-tree value can be
wrong. A negotiable deadline becomes an explicit alternative with its own terms; it does not remove the
timing question. AGY's empirical turnaround suggestion is useful after access, but unfinished tests and
state-dependent delays must remain represented instead of estimating only from completed assays. Keep the
finite independent event-tree oracle; no new lab scheduler or whole-mine POMDP (§22.4, GB-29).

### COD-09 — Comparability frontier

**DESIGN.** Both are right that this is a small consumer of existing definition layers, and it should merge
with the public/JV comparison workflow rather than become an issuer ontology or separate peer database.
CC's proposed JV bridge calibration can reveal the adjustment for that same asset, but does not establish
that the adjustment transfers to another asset, corporate allocation or product mix. Add rank predicates
only when their peer universe, tie policy and full admitted input set support them. A common component must
answer an actual finance question, not merely be the deepest subexpression two incompatible metrics happen
to share. Keep refusals and bounded common views inside `fmine-metrics` (§21.6, BD-060–063/273).

### COD-16 — Action-aware forecasts and causal benefit

**DESIGN.** Both are right that a new DiD implementation or experiment-management product would duplicate
planned work and lack a credible public real-site validation path. I merge action-aware resolution into
COD-08, retaining an explicit link between forecast target, action policy, actual adoption and outcome;
otherwise neither the resolved/non-resolved rule nor the vendor score is well defined. That is stronger than
an unqualified status flag: a forecast already conditional on normal preventive maintenance may resolve
normally, while an unobserved no-action counterfactual cannot be scored from the treated outcome.
I reject generic ABAB setpoint schedules as the default cure for missing controls: carryover, residence time,
feed drift and interference must be addressed in a human-approved design, and safety actions are never
withheld to manufacture a comparison. Real causal benefit remains gated on an admissible design and null
reporting, not inferred from the observed improvement (§16.5, G-CRUC-5, §19.6).

## What I would take forward

**DESIGN.** After the M0 honesty repairs, the first additional public demonstration should be COD-02 on pinned
discrepancies and COD-08 in every released forecast cohort. COD-03 earns its place as an input contract for the
first statistical model; COD-01 needs a real guidance-review win; COD-04 and strict sentence binding attach
to actual reviewed outputs. All remaining extensions wait for a named consumer or fold into already-planned
work. I accept the criticism that this is fewer than fifteen independent new products; that is a better
result of the duel than defending the original packaging.
