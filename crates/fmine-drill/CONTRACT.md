# CONTRACT.md — `fmine-drill`

## 1. Purpose
Corebox drillhole records on the authority ledger (`BD-163`, plan §12.1): collar, survey, interval, sample, dispatch, assay result, QC insertion, lab certificate, sample loss, twin pair, twin acceptance, sample discard and batch flag. A record is written once and corrected only by superseding it with a reason; every version stays readable. A commit-time law refuses a record that names one that is not recorded. Minimum-curvature desurvey turns a collar and its survey stations into a 3-D trace (`BD-164`, plan §12.2), with a positional covariance at any depth from the stations' and collar's stated accuracies under declared error terms (`bd-zx57`), and a sweep-line join finds the overlapping from/to intervals of two sets, hole by hole (`BD-165`). Compositing cuts domain runs into length- or mass-weighted composites that conserve their inputs (`BD-166`, PO-CORE-2). Every dispatched sample is an `OB-LAB` obligation that resolves to a received result or a loss declared with its QA/QC finding (`BD-172`). A twin pair is measured on the desurveyed traces and its matched grades compared; a twin-derived correction enters a public or disclosure-basis estimate only under recorded QP acceptances (`G-TWIN`); and an RC smearing monitor tests mineralized intervals for a down-hole decline (`BD-285`, plan §12.4). The campaign data checks test grade against core recovery, compare drilling programs domain by domain, check in/out survey runs and collars against the DTM, report each program's QA/QC regimes, and select a lab batch committed with a QC failure only by an explicit decision, saying whether its material can still be re-assayed (`BD-286`, plan §12.2, §12.4).

- **Crate:** `fmine-drill`
- **Layer:** K1
- **Subsystem:** Corebox
- **LOC budget:** 15,000 planned / 17,000 ceiling
- **Allowed Module Families:** `["checks", "composite", "desurvey", "join", "lab", "qaqc", "records", "stats", "store", "survey_error", "twin"]`

## 2. Inputs
- Typed records (`records::DrillRecord`) from importers, and corrections with their reasons (`store::Change`).
- An authority-ledger cell: `fmine_ledger::l0::L0Ledger` with its `CellLease` for `store::commit`. For a durable `fmine_ledger::segment::SemanticSegmentWriter`, the caller builds the batch with `store::batch_for` and `store::delta_ops` and appends it with `append_with_law(&batch, store::drill_law)`; this crate does no IO.
- Dependencies: `fmine-core` (codec, digests, portable trigonometry), `fmine-evidence` (refusals), `fmine-claim` (`SpatialSupport`, `AdditivityClass`), `fmine-ledger` and `fmine-runtime` (`ObligationRegistry`, the ingest region) (`K1 -> S0`, `K1 -> S1`). Zero external crates.
- For compositing: domain runs (`composite::Domain`), inputs with their span, value bounds and optional mass (`composite::Input`, `Bounds::of(&AssayValue)`), and a `composite::Rule` (length, weighting, residual handling, additivity, input support).
- Survey errors use an explicit `ErrorModel`; horizontal collar sigma is one standard deviation on each grid axis independently, not a radial confidence radius. The collar errors and angular terms are independent; each angular term declares random station errors or systematic errors shared within an instrument/run.
- CRM assessment (`BD-167`, `BD-282`, plan §12.4) takes a certified mean, certified within-lab σ_C, certified between-lab σ_L, supplied running within-lab S_W, and 2–4096 uncensored results in one declared concentration unit. Warning/failure multipliers and central confidence are explicit policy inputs. The caller supplies analyte, CRM, lab, method and certificate provenance; this kernel does not authenticate them.

## 3. Outputs
- One `DRLR` `FMNE` frame (version 1, CRC32C) per record version, stored at `drill/<kind>/<id>` as a `Put` (first version) or a `Supersede` (a correction with its reason).
- Reads: `store::current`, `store::history` (every version, oldest first, with batch sequence and epoch) and `store::as_of` (the version live after a given number of committed batches).
- `composite::CompositeSet`: composites by depth, each with domain, span, sampled length, weight, value bounds (none when unsampled), `SpatialSupport::Composite { length_mm }` and the inputs it takes; the dropped residual pieces; and the conservation totals with their relative error.
- `join::overlaps` and `join::join_intervals`: every overlapping pair of two span or interval sets with the span they share, sorted by (hole,) left index and right index. `join::self_overlaps`: the overlapping pairs within one set.
- `twin::TwinAssessment`: per matched pair the midpoint separation (m) and whether either midpoint is extrapolated; the pair's largest separation and `TwinState`; `CV_AVR%` with the pairs used and left out; the RMA slope; and the paired t-test (mean and sd of twin − original, t, degrees of freedom, two-sided p). `twin::Admission` for an admitted correction. `twin::SmearingReport`: per mineralized interval the sample count, Mann–Kendall `S`, `z`, one-sided p and state, with the proposals.
- `desurvey::Trace` from `desurvey::desurvey(&Collar, &[Survey])`: each station's point in the collar's CRS, and `Trace::at(depth_mm)`, the point and direction at any depth, marked `extrapolated` above the first station or below the last.
- `UncertainTrace::checked_covariance_at` returns finite total, collar and per-term east/north/up covariance at a queried depth. `checked_variance_along` projects each independent sensitivity before squaring; zero-variance directions remain nonnegative without clamping a matrix cancellation residual. `twin::separation_uncertainty` uses these projections and leaves the nominal twin classification unchanged.
- `qaqc::CrmAssessment` returns observed mean, sample SD and standard error; signed bias, its magnitude and Student-t interval; ordered per-result single-value checks on all three SD bases; and the separate CANMET outcome. Warning counts include failures, while each result has one exclusive pass/warning/failure state. Every supplied observation remains represented by its original index.

## 4. Invariants
- **CRM rules remain distinct (DESIGN, `BD-282`):** strict `|X − μ| > k × SD` exceedances use each named SD unchanged; computed binary64 equality passes that boundary, with no assumed decimal rounding tolerance. CANMET uses `|mean − μ| <= 2 sqrt(σ_L² + S_W²/n)`, never the observed sample SD in place of supplied S_W. A synthetic ten-result batch gives 10 warnings/8 failures on σ_C, 3/0 on σ_L, 4/0 on S_W and a CANMET pass, while retaining the positive bias and its interval (`qaqc::named_sd_bases_and_canmet_retain_their_different_known_answers`).
- **Supersede, never edit (`INV-003`, plan §8.1):** a `Put` over a recorded key and a `Supersede` of an unrecorded key are refused by the ledger; every earlier version keeps its bytes and stays readable as of its time (`supersession::test_corrections_supersede_and_every_version_stays_readable`, `supersession::test_an_edit_in_place_is_refused_and_changes_nothing`).
- **A correction says why:** a `Supersede` carries a non-empty reason and a `Put` carries none.
- **No dangling names:** a survey or interval names a recorded collar; a sample its intervals; a dispatch its samples; a result its sample, dispatch and certificate; a QC insertion its sample and, for a duplicate or umpire, the original. A record and what it names may arrive in one batch. Ids never disappear, so the names stay good after any correction.
- **Result and dispatch agree:** a result's dispatch lists its sample and went to the lab that reported it. A dispatch correction cannot change the lab or drop a sample, so this survives corrections.
- **No silent missing sample (`OB-LAB`, plan §12.4):** `lab::LabBook` folds the cell's batches in order. A dispatch opens one obligation per sample at its dispatch time, and a correction adding a sample opens one. The first result resolves it at its receipt time; a `SampleLoss` aborts it with its finding. The ingest region cannot close while one is open (`PO-BED-5`). The law refuses a result or loss dated before its dispatch, a loss of a sample its dispatch does not list, and a correction that moves a result or loss to another sample or dispatch. A loss after a result, a result after a loss, and a repeated loss are kept as `LabConflict`s, and the obligation stays as it first closed (`lab_obligations::test_each_dispatched_sample_resolves_to_a_result_or_a_declared_loss`, `supersession::test_the_law_refuses_dangling_and_inconsistent_records_and_writes_nothing`).
- **Obligation replay:** the fold equals `ObligationRegistry::replay` of its own journal, the fold of the same batches read back from a segment written through the law, the fold resumed after any prefix, and a `DurableObligationRegistry` reopened from disk (`lab_obligations::test_the_fold_replays_from_its_journal_from_disk_and_batch_by_batch`, `lab_obligations::test_a_durable_registry_reopens_to_the_fold_and_guards_the_ingest_region`).
- **QC material is not core:** a CRM or blank sits on a `QcMaterial` sample, which is taken from no interval; a duplicate or umpire names drilled samples on both sides. A sample correction cannot change which kind it is.
- **A weak twin is weak at depth (`BD-285`, plan §12.1):** a pair's separation is the largest distance between its matched intervals' midpoints on the two desurveyed traces. Above the policy limit (default 5 m) the pair is a `WeakTwin`; at the limit it is a twin. A twin collared 2 m away and dipping 85° is 2.96 m away at 11 m and 10.8 m at 101 m, so it is weak (`twin::test_a_twin_more_than_five_metres_away_on_its_trace_is_a_weak_twin`).
- **`G-TWIN` (G-CORE-1, E377):** a twin-derived correction is admitted to a `Full`-basis internal projection; to a `Public` or disclosure-basis estimate only when the `TwinAcceptance`s it cites are recorded in the cell and together name every pair it uses. The law refuses an acceptance of an unrecorded pair, a twin side matched to another hole's interval, and a correction that changes the holes a pair compares (`twin::test_a_twin_correction_enters_a_disclosure_basis_estimate_only_under_recorded_acceptances`, `twin::test_the_law_refuses_twins_of_unrecorded_or_wrong_records_and_moved_holes`).
- **RC smearing monitor (E391):** a Mann–Kendall test down each mineralized interval of an RC hole, on the samples wholly inside it; a one-sided p below the policy level (default 5%, at least 10 samples) proposes a diamond twin. On a seeded synthetic campaign it fires on 400 of 400 smeared holes and 15 of 400 clean ones (`twin::test_the_smearing_monitor_fires_on_a_seeded_smeared_campaign_and_rarely_on_a_clean_one`).
- **Recovery–grade bias test (`BD-286`, E378):** Spearman's ρ of grade against core recovery per (domain, program, drilling technique, recovery method); recovery that is missing or of unknown method is left out and listed. A two-sided p below the policy level (default 5%, at least 10 intervals) marks the group's intervals `Conflicted`. On a seeded synthetic campaign of three 12-hole programs, a planted dependence in one program's fresh rock gives ρ 0.92 and fires on 60 of 60 seeds; clean groups fire on 17 of 300 (`campaign_checks::test_each_check_fires_on_its_planted_defect_in_a_seeded_campaign`, `campaign_checks::test_the_statistical_checks_hold_their_level_and_find_their_defects_across_seeds`).
- **Data-generation comparison (E379):** every pair of programs in a domain, by mean, Q–Q points at the nine deciles and a two-sided Mann–Whitney test. Pairwise, so one biased program does not move a pooled reference; a program is named for review only when it differs from every other tested program of its domain. Oxide grades 1.6 times the others' differ on 120 of 120 seeded pairs; clean pairs differ on 7 of 240, and a single chance difference names no program.
- **Survey and collar checks (E381):** at each depth surveyed both going in and coming out, the dip difference and, below 85° dip, the azimuth difference wrapped through north, each against `k` (default 3) times the two stations' combined one-sigma; the runs' separation is measured on their desurveyed traces at the deepest shared depth, and `run_separation` tests it against `k` times its one-sigma from the two runs' survey-error covariances, the shared collar cancelling. Seeded hole D07, its out-run 3° steeper from 40 m, is 1.668 m apart at 60 m against σ 0.195 m, ratio 2.85, and is the only hole flagged. A collar's surveyed elevation minus the DTM's is compared with `k` times their combined one-sigma. Both have hand-worked answers: limits of 1.06° dip, 2.12° azimuth and 0.949 m, a 0.8° wrap, and the minimum-curvature separation of a vertical in-run and a 1° out-run at 100 m (`campaign_checks::test_the_survey_and_collar_checks_have_their_hand_worked_answers`).
- **Regimes and flagged batches (E223, E224):** each program's results are counted by declared `qaqc_regime`, and a program that mixes regimes is reported. Every result of a flagged lab batch is listed as flagged whether the estimate includes or excludes them; there is no default decision. A flagged result whose sample was discarded after QC is `Reassay::Impossible`.
- **Discarded material is not sent (E224):** the law refuses a dispatch that newly lists a sample whose `SampleDiscard`, staged or recorded, is dated at or before the dispatch. A dispatch dated before the discard, or a correction keeping a sample its dispatch already listed, is admitted (`campaign_checks::test_the_law_refuses_a_dispatch_of_material_discarded_after_qc`).
- **One encoding per version:** decoding refuses bytes that are not exactly what the encoder writes for the record they hold (for example `-0.0`, or trailing bytes).
- **Same law on both rungs:** `store::drill_law` runs in `L0Ledger::commit_with_conservation` and `SemanticSegmentWriter::append_with_law`; both admit and refuse the same batches and reach the same head (`supersession::test_the_law_refuses_dangling_and_inconsistent_records_and_writes_nothing`).
- **Desurvey is minimum curvature (PO-CORE-1):** between stations the hole is the circular arc joining their directions. It is within 1 mm per 1,000 m of exact positions on holes built from arcs and straight runs, at every 250 mm and every station, and of the textbook inclination-and-azimuth form on 60 seeded random 2 km holes. Measured: 1.9 nm from the geometry, the rounding of UTM coordinates, and 7e-11 m per 1,000 m from the textbook. The balanced-tangential control (ratio factor 1) is 4.5 m per 1,000 m off, so the comparison detects a wrong method (`desurvey::test_holes_of_arcs_and_straight_runs_desurvey_to_their_true_positions`, `desurvey::test_random_two_kilometre_holes_match_the_textbook_form`).
- **Survey-error covariance (`bd-zx57`, plan §12.2):** `survey_error::propagate` attaches an error model of declared terms to a desurveyed hole. Each term takes a share of one angle's stated variance, at random per station or systematic per instrument and run, and an angle's shares sum to 1. The collar's horizontal and vertical sigmas add independently. The covariance at any depth is the linear propagation through the minimum-curvature legs that `Trace::at` uses, including the runs above the first station and below the last. On straight holes, at stations, inside legs, above the first station and below the last, under random, systematic (two runs) and mixed models, it equals the closed form `Σ wₖ²σ²` and `(Σ wₖ)²σ²` per run to 2e-11 of the trace; along the hole only the collar's accuracy is left (`survey_error::test_a_straight_hole_has_its_closed_form_covariance_at_stations_and_between_them`). On a 400 m hole that steepens and turns, with two instruments and a mixed model, 20,000 perturbed surveys desurveyed by `desurvey` agree with it within 1.74 standard errors over 54 elements at six depths. The control, which takes the systematic shares as random, is 539 standard errors off (`survey_error::test_a_curved_hole_matches_a_monte_carlo_of_perturbed_surveys`).
- **A twin's separation has its sigma:** `twin::separation_uncertainty` gives each matched pair of an assessment the one-sigma of its separation along the line between the midpoints, from both holes' covariances taken as independent, and `Φ((d − limit)/σ)`, the chance that the true separation is beyond the limit. It refuses an assessment that the traces offered do not reproduce bit for bit. Parallel holes dipping 60° and offset 2.0, 4.9 and 5.2 m have σ = 0.1810 m, the closed form, and a twin 4.9 m away is beyond 5 m with probability 0.29 (`twin::test_a_twin_separation_carries_the_two_holes_survey_error`).
- **A station on the trace changes nothing:** a station added at a depth with the trace's own direction there moves no point by more than 10 nm, and the order of the input stations does not matter (`desurvey::test_a_station_added_on_the_trace_moves_nothing_and_input_order_does_not_matter`).
- **Compositing conserves (PO-CORE-2):** Σ weight × value over the composites, dropped pieces included, equals the in-domain inputs' total from a separate input-to-domain join to 1e-12 relative, or the set is refused. On 3,200 seeded random sets (200 holes; every residual rule, both weightings, intensive and extensive) the worst was 2.5e-16. Values equal a pair-by-pair recomputation to 5.6e-16, and composites tile every domain with no gap, overlap or boundary crossing. The control, the total without the dropped pieces, is off by 0.10 (`composite::test_seeded_random_holes_conserve_tile_their_domains_and_match_brute_force`).
- **Composites have known values:** hand-worked binary-fraction cases for length and mass weighting, domain contacts, each residual rule, unsampled core (no value, not zero), censored inputs (bounds, no substitution) and extensive quantities (`composite::test_length_weighted_composites_have_their_known_values_and_respect_domains`, `composite::test_each_residual_rule_gives_its_known_pieces`, `composite::test_unsampled_core_mass_weighting_censoring_and_extensive_quantities`).
- **The join is the pair-by-pair answer:** on 320 seeded random cases (970,252 overlapping pairs) of dense, sparse, gridded, nested and identical spans, `overlaps` and `self_overlaps` equal brute force. Spans that only touch do not overlap, and swapping the sides swaps every pair (`join::test_the_sweep_matches_brute_force_on_seeded_random_spans`, `join::test_touching_nested_identical_and_shared_start_spans`). Records on different holes never pair (`join::test_records_join_hole_by_hole`).
- **No assumed straight hole:** a hole with no surveyed station, or declared unsurveyed, is refused. Above the first station and below the last, a measured direction is extended and the point says so (`desurvey::test_beyond_the_stations_the_hole_runs_straight_and_says_so`, `desurvey::test_holes_that_cannot_be_desurveyed_are_refused`).
- Records that cannot be true alone are refused before any write: an interval whose `to` is not past its `from`, an azimuth outside [0, 360), a dip outside [−90, 90], an assay mass fraction outside [0, 1], a "no survey" station with an orientation, a collar with no position or with planned and actual positions in two CRSs.

## 5. Refusals
- `DrillError::Invalid` → `FMINE-REF-109` (a field outside what the record allows; two changes to one record in one batch; a join span whose `to_mm` is not past its `from_mm`; compositing with a zero length, overlapping domains or inputs, bounds that are not finite or ordered, or a missing mass under mass weighting; a hole that cannot be desurveyed: no surveyed station, declared unsurveyed with surveyed stations, a station of another hole, two stations at one depth, consecutive stations in opposite directions).
- An error model with an empty or repeated code, a share outside (0, 1], or an angle whose shares do not sum to 1 is `DrillError::Invalid` → `FMINE-REF-109` (`survey_error::test_a_model_that_does_not_use_each_sigma_whole_is_refused`).
- Checked covariance and projection queries refuse nonfinite arithmetic, including squared-sigma or extrapolation overflow. Twin separation uncertainty also refuses a nonfinite combined variance, an invalid limit, different CRSs, or coincident midpoints, where the distance derivative is undefined; the nominal assessment remains available.
- CRM checks refuse nonfinite or negative concentrations/SDs, a batch outside 2–4096 observations, multipliers not satisfying `0 < warning_k < failure_k`, confidence outside representable numerical resolution, and nonfinite computed limits/endpoints or loss of a nonzero uncertainty term (`DrillError::Invalid` → `FMINE-REF-109`). No result is dropped to complete a batch.
- `DrillError::CrsMismatch` → `FMINE-REF-106` (planned and actual collar positions in different CRSs).
- `DrillError::Corrupt` → `FMINE-REF-505` (bytes that are not one canonical record version).
- `lab::LabError::WrongCell` → `FMINE-REF-109`, `OutOfOrder` → `FMINE-REF-502`, `Obligation` → `FMINE-REF-505` (a transition the registry refuses, such as a result for a sample no dispatch sent, which only a batch admitted without the law can hold); `Record` keeps its own code (`lab_obligations::test_a_batch_out_of_order_of_another_cell_or_past_the_law_is_refused`).
- `DrillError::MissingAcceptance` → `FMINE-REF-304` (`G-TWIN`: a twin-derived correction offered to a public or disclosure-basis estimate citing no acceptance, an unrecorded one, or acceptances that leave a pair uncovered). A correction from an unrecorded pair, a twin assessment given traces, intervals or grades that do not belong, and a smearing check of a hole that is not RC, with overlapping spans or with a policy out of range are `DrillError::Invalid` → `FMINE-REF-109`.
- The campaign checks refuse, as `DrillError::Invalid` → `FMINE-REF-109`, a policy out of range, a repeated collar, interval or sample, an interval of a hole not given, a sample of intervals not given or in two holes, an interval graded twice or with a negative or non-finite grade, a survey of another hole, a result on a sample not given, and a DTM elevation or accuracy that is not finite or a negative accuracy (`campaign_checks::test_the_index_and_grades_refuse_what_does_not_belong`).
- `DrillError::SupportMismatch` → `FMINE-REF-102` (compositing inputs that are neither core samples nor composites).
- `DrillError::NonAdditive` → `FMINE-REF-103` (compositing a non-additive quantity, `INV-014`).
- `DrillError::Conservation` → `FMINE-REF-101` (a composite set that does not conserve its inputs to 1e-12 relative).
- Ledger refusals keep the ledger's codes: `KeyAlreadyExists` → `FMINE-REF-502`, `KeyNotFound` → `FMINE-REF-201`, and the law's violations (`ConservationViolation`) → `FMINE-REF-101`.

## 6. Determinism class
- `qaqc` uses sorted, offset-and-scaled compensated moment reductions and deterministic Student-t tail inversion; row results retain input order. Permuting observations changes their indices, not the reported moments or thresholds.
`BitwisePortable` (plan §29.2): integer, byte and ordered-map operations; records compare and store floats as IEEE-754 bits. Desurvey uses IEEE-754 arithmetic and `fmine_core::det` trigonometry and square roots, no std transcendental calls. `join` is `Exact`: integer depths, sorting and comparisons. `composite` uses IEEE-754 addition, multiplication and division with Neumaier compensated sums. `twin` uses IEEE-754 arithmetic with `fmine_core::det` square roots, hypotenuses, logarithms, exponentials and the normal CDF; its t p-values come from a Lanczos log-gamma and the incomplete-beta continued fraction. `survey_error` uses IEEE-754 arithmetic and `fmine_core::det` sines, cosines and square roots, with central differences of fixed step on offsets from the collar. `stats` and `checks` use IEEE-754 arithmetic and `fmine_core::det`; ranks sort by total order, and the normal tail below z = −3 is Laplace's continued fraction, where `1 + erf` cancels.

## 7. Evidence class of outputs
Records as imported: layer L0 Evidence (plan §13.2). This crate assigns no evidence class; an interval's logger (human, scanner, model) is kept so that later stages can.

## 8. Validity domain
- CRM bias intervals are fixed-batch, two-sided Student-t intervals for `mean − certified_mean` using observed sample SD and `n − 1` degrees of freedom. They assume independent, identically distributed normal results and a fixed reference mean. Certificate uncertainty, serial dependence and systematic errors are excluded from this conditional interval; a zero observed SD collapses it without establishing zero lab uncertainty. A rule pass is not a calibration, compliance or release finding.
- Depths along the hole in integer millimetres; positions in a registered CRS with one-sigma accuracies; assay values as mass fractions, censored values kept as bounds.
- Compositing: composites start at the top of each domain run and never cross its base. An input's mass and value are taken as spread evenly along it. Core outside every domain is not composited. A composite of non-uniform length (a residual or merged piece) carries its own length as its support.
- Desurvey: azimuths are bearings from the collar CRS's grid north; magnetic or true north is converted by the importer. One run per depth: in and out runs at the same depth are refused until the caller picks one. The collar's actual position is used, or the planned one when there is no actual (`Trace::collar_from`).
- Survey covariance is a first-order angular model, not a qualified ISCWSA instrument model. Large angular errors or nearly opposing directions can invalidate its linearization. Twin probabilities additionally assume Gaussian errors, independent holes and positional uncertainty small relative to their positive nominal separation; zero separation has no first-order distance probability.
- Campaign checks: a program is a collar's `program`. Grades are the caller's, one unit per call, coded to domains by the caller. A DTM elevation at a collar is supplied with its accuracy; nothing here samples a surface. In/out runs pair stations at exactly the same depth.
- Records are checked alone and by name. The commit-time law does not check depths against the hole (an interval below the last survey) or interval overlap per logger. `desurvey` and `join::self_overlaps` give the answers; no write is refused on them yet.

## 9. Oracles
- CRM checks: independently summed synthetic mean/variance and known SD-basis disagreement; equality and zero-SD controls; closed-form Student-t quantiles for one and two degrees of freedom; permutation, unit-scaling and large-offset moment controls (`tests/qaqc.rs`).
- Survey error: the first-order closed form of a straight hole (station weights of half each adjoining leg, the run from the collar, `f·h·(2 − f)/2` and `f²·h/2` inside a leg) and a Monte Carlo of surveys perturbed as the model declares, desurveyed by the independent `desurvey` path, with an all-random control it must reject.
- Twins: separations of vertical and inclined straight holes worked by hand; `CV_AVR%`, RMA slope and the paired t-test on hand-worked pairs; t p-values against the closed-form CDFs for 1 to 5 degrees of freedom (worst 2.3e-14 on 5,000 seeded values each), published critical values to 1e-9 and the normal limit; Mann–Kendall `S` and `z` on hand-worked declining, constant and tied runs; and a seeded synthetic RC campaign.
- Campaign checks: Spearman's ρ = −29/165 with p 0.627188 on a published ten-subject example; a Mann–Whitney U equal to the hand count of pairs; the normal tail to 1e-12 relative of published Φ(−3), Φ(−5), Φ(−10), Φ(−20); hand-worked survey, separation and DTM limits; and a seeded synthetic campaign with one planted defect per check.
- Compositing: hand-worked binary-fraction answers, compared exactly, and a pair-by-pair brute-force recomputation of every composite value on seeded random holes.
- Join: the brute-force pair-by-pair overlap of the two sets, and a hand-worked case of touching, nested, identical and shared-start spans.
- Desurvey: exact positions of holes made of circular arcs and straight runs (minimum curvature is exact on an arc), and the textbook inclination-and-azimuth form (as in Bourgoyne et al., *Applied Drilling Engineering*, SPE, 1986) written in the test with std trigonometry and an arc-cosine dogleg.
- Hand-built records and frames in the unit tests, including frames written with the record writer but holding values the checks refuse, and a `-0.0` frame resealed with a valid CRC32C.
- The ledger's own refusals (`fmine-ledger`), observed on both rungs.

## 10. Known gaps
- The fixed CRM evaluator does not implement versioned site-specific rule text, sequential pattern or e-process monitoring, blanks, duplicate/umpire analysis, investigation creation or automatic batch-flag propagation. Those remain separate parts of `BD-167`, `BD-282` and `BD-283`.
- No importer writes these records yet (acQuire dialect, `BD-188`).
- MNPI embargo state on results is not in the record (`BD-170`); a later frame version adds it.
- A certificate record names its original by SHA-256 and by the `FMT-CAS-1` root of its copy (`cas_root`). This crate does no IO: storing the original and checking the root against the digest happen at ingest (`BD-171`).
- `OB-LAB` has no deadline: an outstanding sample is listed, but nothing marks it overdue against a lab's turnaround. The law does not refuse a loss after a result, or a result after a loss: finding one would need a by-sample index. They are reported as conflicts instead.
- `Structure`, `Density`, `GeometTest` and `GeotechLog` (plan §12.1) are not here yet.
- A twin study is not yet a Claim over its pairs (plan §12.1, E375, E376): `twin::assess_twin` reports one pair. The caller supplies the matched grades in one unit; nothing groups a hole's results into the matched intervals. The `G-TWIN` guard checks that the cited acceptances are recorded and cover every pair, not that the role named holds a QP's authority.
- The smearing monitor returns its findings and proposals and opens no InvestigationCase: `fmine-calib`'s cases take forecast monitors (coverage, CUSUM) only. Its Mann–Kendall test uses the normal approximation with the tie correction and no correction for serial correlation along the hole.
- The campaign checks report findings and open no InvestigationCase, and a `Conflicted` interval is a returned list, not a stored state. Each group and pair is tested at the policy level with no correction for the number of tests, so a campaign with many domains expects some chance findings. The comparison is of raw intervals: programs that drilled different parts of a domain will differ, and nothing declusters. Tests assume independent intervals; grades correlated along a hole make p-values too small. Flags join results by lab and lab batch only.
- The survey-error model has angle and collar terms only: no along-hole depth error, no tool misalignment, declination or magnetic-interference terms of their own, and no global term shared across runs. Every term scales with each station's stated sigma. The propagation is first order. The in/out run separation takes the two runs' errors as independent, so an error the in and out runs of one tool share is counted twice and the test is conservative. A twin's separation sigma takes the two holes' errors as independent: collars surveyed from one base station, or holes surveyed by one tool, would be correlated. The declared straight-line fallback for unsurveyed short holes needs a planned collar orientation, which `Collar` does not carry.
- No lab or vendor desurvey output is in the oracles yet (PO-CORE-1 names a lab oracle).
- Compositing takes spans and values, not records: grouping a hole's samples and results into inputs, and coding intervals to domains from logging or wireframes, are left to the caller. There is no bulk-density weighting beyond a given input mass, and no compositing across holes.
